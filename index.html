Our Legacy

Building the Future of Romania

Alexandra Becali Imobiliare S.R.L. is a premier construction firm based in Bucharest, Romania. We specialize in the high-end residential building sector, operating as a comprehensive developer and general contractor. Our approach integrates traditional craftsmanship with modern engineering to deliver living spaces that define contemporary urban excellence.

With deep roots in the Sector 1 district, we manage the entire lifecycle of construction—from strategic land subdivision and foundation engineering to the final architectural exterior finishes. Our commitment to quality ensures that every project contributes to the sustainable growth of our capital's skyline.
